What it is

A free invoice generator for freelancers and small businesses, tuned for Indian GST but usable for any currency. Add line items with live totals, apply GST-aware tax, pick a currency, add your logo, and print a clean PDF — all without an account.

Key features

  • Line items with description, qty, rate, per-line tax %, live amount
  • Auto totals — subtotal, discount, tax, shipping, grand total
  • GST modes — No tax · CGST+SGST (intra-state) · IGST (inter-state), with tax breakup
  • 9 currencies — INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, AUD, CAD, SGD, JPY (locale-correct)
  • Grand total in words — INR lakh/crore scale, western scale for others
  • Your logo, business + client details incl. GSTIN
  • Print → PDF, plus export / import invoice as JSON
  • Autosaves to your browser; optional AI to draft terms / line wording

Tech

Client-only Astro + React 19 islands + Tailwind v4. Optional AI via @chirag127/oz-ai (g4f, keyless). PWA-installable and 100% in-browser — no upload, no signup, no server. Invoice data stays in your browser's localStorage.
